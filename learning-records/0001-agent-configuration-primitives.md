# Understanding agent configuration primitives

## Change

I understand and actively use rules and skills to shape AI-agent behavior. Skills are my most frequently used mechanism. I understand hooks, but I do not use them daily because agent-activation behavior can sometimes fail to align with my intent even when the trigger appears correct. I understand sub-agents, but I have not found a compelling best use case and generally choose skills instead.

## Evidence

This baseline comes from my stated understanding and ongoing use of rules and skills, together with my experience evaluating hook activation and sub-agent delegation.

## Impact

Future AI sessions should treat rules and skills as my default mechanisms. They should not recommend hooks or sub-agents by default; recommendations should explain the concrete benefit and the activation or delegation trade-off.
