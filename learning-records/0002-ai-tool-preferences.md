# Current AI tool preferences and experience

## Change

I have an established working preference across several AI tools:

- **Codex:** Frequently used and currently my favorite.
- **Claude Code:** Used frequently in early 2025 and preferred as a CLI-style tool. Since app-based tools such as Codex became available, I lean more toward Codex.
- **Kiro:** Used because of an employer-provided limit; the overall experience is positive.
- **Hermes:** Works well, but can sometimes feel over-engineered.
- **OpenCode:** Works well, but often times out.
- **OpenRouter:** Very valuable because of its flexibility.
- **Antigravity:** My favorite plan-mode experience, but its harness quality is disappointing.
- **Cursor:** My first go-to AI IDE. I no longer use it because of cost; at the $20 price point, I would choose Codex instead.
- **9router:** I understand it but rarely use it because the relevant constraint is currently Kiro's limit, and token savings are not very useful when using Luna, which feels effectively unlimited.

## Evidence

This record is based on my stated usage frequency, preferences, constraints, and observed trade-offs across these tools.

## Impact

Future AI sessions should treat Codex as my default recommendation when a general-purpose tool is needed, OpenRouter as a strong option when model flexibility matters, and the other tools with their stated caveats. Do not assume that Cursor is still part of my active workflow.
